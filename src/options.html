Privacy at a glance

  • Username, repository snapshot, baseline and settings stay in this profile.
  • Website mode lets the browser attach your github.com session cookies; StarBoard never reads or stores the cookie values.
  • API tokens are session-only by default, go only to api.github.com, and are never included in diagnostics or exports.
  • No analytics, developer telemetry, advertising or third-party data sharing.
